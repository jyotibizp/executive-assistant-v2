# Rhythms

## Daily brief — `brief`
1. **Today's calendar** — each meeting + what to prepare
2. **Top 3** — tied to a vertical and to `context/priorities.md`
3. **Replies owed** — emails / follow-ups waiting on Pradeep
4. **One risk or blocker** to watch
5. Monday only: Eternal Legends upload check

## Weekly review — `weekly`
1. Progress per vertical vs P1
2. What slipped and why (one line each)
3. Open loops still open
4. Decisions made → lines for `logs/decisions-log.md`
5. Next week's top 5

## Monthly reset — `monthly`
1. Review each `context/verticals/` file — status still true?
2. Propose edits to `context/priorities.md` (exact text)
3. What to park, what to promote
4. People to re-contact from `context/people.md`

## Weekly anchors
- **Monday** — YouTube episode upload
- OPEN: trading hours, deep-work blocks, meeting days
